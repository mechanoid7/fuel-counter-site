Everything You Need to Manage Your Vehicle

Built for drivers who value simplicity, privacy, and actionable statistics.

Fuel & Expense Tracking

Quickly record fill-ups with liters or gallons, price per unit, total cost, odometer reading, and full tank tracking.

Smart Analytics

Gain insights with consumption rates (L/100km, MPG, km/L), operational cost per kilometer/mile, and monthly averages.

Multi-Vehicle Garage

Manage multiple cars and motorcycles in a single profile. Switch between vehicles with one tap and track each independently.

Google Drive Backup

Bidirectional cloud sync keeps your database safe on your private Google Drive with seamless two-way merge across devices.

Privacy & Offline First

All your data is stored locally in an embedded SQLite database. No mandatory account registration and full offline support.

Currencies & Units

Seamlessly switch between Kilometers and Miles, Liters and Gallons, and manage multi-currency fill-ups with exchange rates.
