Travel & Road Trips

Crossing borders? No need for mental math at the pump.

Driving through another country often means dealing with foreign currencies, unfamiliar units, and spotty mobile roaming. Fuel Counter removes the friction from travel fill-ups:

Log in Local Currency

Enter the exact numbers from the station receipt — EUR in Germany, PLN in Poland, or USD in the States. Each entry preserves its original currency and price.

Automatic Conversion

All dashboard metrics, monthly averages, and cost-per-km automatically convert into your home currency, giving you a clear picture of total trip spending.

Zero Roaming Needed

Exchange rates for 160+ currencies are stored right inside your local SQLite database. Everything works seamlessly on highways and mountain passes without internet.
